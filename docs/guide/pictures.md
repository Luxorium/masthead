# Pictures

[Back to the guide](README.md)

You can show the agents pictures, such as a screenshot of an error or a sketch of a page.

## Attaching a picture

1. Do one of these:
   - Press **Attach pictures** next to the message box and choose one or more files.
   - Paste a picture into the message box (for example a screenshot you just copied).
   - Drag a picture onto the message box.
2. The picture shows above the box. **Remove picture** takes it off again.
3. Write your message and press **Send**.

## Limits

- PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP pictures only. For other files (a PDF, say), put the file in the chat's
  folder and mention its name in your message.
- At most 8 pictures per message, and 30 MB of pictures in total.
- Large pictures are shrunk for you. A picture over 40 MB, or one still over 5 MB after shrinking,
  cannot be attached: use a smaller one.

## Seeing pictures

Click a picture in the chat to open it large. **Previous picture** and **Next picture** move between
them, **Open in a new tab** opens it in its own tab, and **Close the picture** returns to the chat.
Pictures that a tool made are marked **Picture from a tool**.
